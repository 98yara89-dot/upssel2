WHAT USUALLY HAPPENS

You may start too quickly, slow down without noticing, rush through a difficult passage or lose your place when a rest appears. Sometimes the notes are correct, but the timing feels uncertain.

Without a steady pulse, it becomes difficult to keep a tune organized. And when you do not understand how beats are grouped, Irish dance rhythms can feel confusing.

WHY RHYTHM NEEDS ITS OWN PRACTICE

Rhythm is more than playing fast or following a beat. It involves pulse, tempo, counting, note duration, rests, subdivisions and accents. Once you understand how these elements work together, you can practise with more control.

The Whistle Rhythm Map takes you from feeling a steady pulse to recognizing common Irish rhythms, practising patterns and transferring them to your tin whistle.
